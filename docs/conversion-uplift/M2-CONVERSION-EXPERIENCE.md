# M2 — Conversion Experience

**Scope:** Phase 2 (homepage conversion uplift) and Phase 3 (service discovery and landing pages).

## Visitor journey

1. The existing homepage hero and portfolio sections introduce the work and the person behind it.
2. The new service-discovery section offers six clear routes based on the visitor's problem area.
3. The service overview lets visitors compare the available areas before choosing one.
4. Each service detail page explains the problem fit, potential outcomes, approach and indicative deliverables.
5. Contextual enquiry links open an email addressed to `hempongroup@gmail.com`, with the selected service in the subject and a lightweight prompt in the message body.
6. The project archive remains available as supporting proof, and existing product pages remain accessible through the Products navigation.

## Service catalogue

- Website Development — business websites, service landing pages and content-rich web platforms.
- Custom Software & Business Systems — workflow-focused applications, dashboards, records and reporting.
- Mobile App Development — cross-platform mobile experiences, including connectivity-aware workflows where relevant.
- Business Automation — scripts, integrations, notifications and repeatable process improvements.
- AI Solutions & Integrations — scoped AI assistance with validation, privacy considerations and human oversight.
- Digital Product Design — user journeys, information architecture, interface design and prototypes.

Descriptions are intentionally framed as service capabilities and potential outcomes, not guaranteed commercial results or unverified client case studies.

## Conversion decisions

- One primary business enquiry destination: `hempongroup@gmail.com`.
- Founder correspondence remains separately available through `magatijoel@gmail.com` in the homepage contact section and site footer.
- No WhatsApp or telephone CTA is added because no verified business number has been supplied.
- No unsupported form backend, CRM, booking workflow or analytics integration is implied.
- Service detail pages include a contextual mailto subject/body to reduce the effort required to make a useful first enquiry.

## Preservation

The homepage hero and existing sections are retained. The new service discovery section is inserted after selected work and before the product showcase. The existing capabilities section remains, with each capability linked to its corresponding service page. Existing projects, posts, product pages, user guides, downloads and update metadata routes are not intentionally changed by M2.
